# Cretes Community Code of Conduct

## Scope and expectations

This original project policy applies to Cretes repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions and official project events, and to people representing the project publicly.

Treat people respectfully regardless of identity, background, experience or opinion. Critique ideas with evidence, welcome questions, respect privacy, and give credit for others' work. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, sustained personal attacks and deliberate disruption are unacceptable.

## Reporting

Send reports privately to creteslang@gmail.com with the subject `Cretes conduct report`. Include relevant links, dates, a description and your preferred contact method. Share only necessary information. Do not publish another person's personal information or a sensitive report in a public issue.

The initial reviewer is @krishanth7. Reports are handled as confidentially as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. The reviewer may request clarification and share necessary details with an appropriate reviewer, explaining the need where practical.

## Enforcement

Consider severity, evidence, impact and repeated behavior. Responses may include a private correction, warning, removal of content, temporary restriction or permanent exclusion. Communicate the reason and duration where appropriate, and allow a response. Immediate restrictions may be used to address credible threats or severe ongoing harm.

An affected person may request reconsideration by the same email with new evidence or a procedural concern. If the lead is involved, request an independent reviewer; no independent enforcement team currently exists. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is prohibited.
